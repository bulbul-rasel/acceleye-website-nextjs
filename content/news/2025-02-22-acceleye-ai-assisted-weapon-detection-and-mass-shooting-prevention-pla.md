AccelEye's AI-assisted weapon detection and mass-shooting prevention platform is now integrated with AxxonSoft's Intelligent video management system (VMS).

Customers who manage their cameras with AxxonSoft can now use AccelEye's detection on those video streams. The AI looks for possible weapons, and AccelEye's 24/7 human verification team confirms each detection before an alert is delivered.

This adds AxxonSoft to the video management platforms that work with AccelEye and makes it easier for organizations to add detection to the system they already use.

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out.
