AccelEye has achieved Minority Business Enterprise (MBE) certification from the National Minority Supplier Development Council (NMSDC).

NMSDC certification is recognized by many corporations, government agencies and school districts that look to work with certified minority-owned businesses. For AccelEye, it is a milestone that supports our mission of making schools and public places safer with AI-assisted weapon detection and 24/7 human verification.

We thank everyone who supported us along the way and look forward to serving more communities. [Contact us](/contact) to learn more.

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out.
