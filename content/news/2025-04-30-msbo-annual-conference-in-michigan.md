AccelEye attended the MSBO Annual Conference in Michigan.

The team demonstrated its AI-assisted weapon detection and mass shooting prevention solution for school business officials and other education leaders, and talked about how schools can add detection to their existing camera systems with human verification behind every alert.

Thank you to the organizers and to everyone who visited our table. To arrange a demo for your district, [contact us](/contact).

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out.
