AccelEye's AI technology detected a gun in a school library in California, in about 90 milliseconds, and demonstrated how early detection can prevent a potential disaster.

The system flagged the weapon almost instantly. Because every detection is reviewed by AccelEye's human verification team, the alert reached the people who needed it with confirmation that the threat was real.

Detection speed matters because every second counts in a school emergency. Earlier warning gives staff time to lock down and gives first responders more time to arrive. You can read more in our [blog post on this incident](/blog/gun-detected-in-school-library).
