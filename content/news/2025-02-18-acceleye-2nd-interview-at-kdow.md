AccelEye took part in a second interview with KDOW about deploying its weapon detection system in schools.

The conversation covered why early detection matters, how AI-assisted detection works with the cameras a school already has, and why a human verification step is part of every alert so that responders are told about real threats and not false alarms.

AccelEye appreciates the chance to talk with local media about school safety and will keep sharing updates as more campuses adopt the technology.

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out.
