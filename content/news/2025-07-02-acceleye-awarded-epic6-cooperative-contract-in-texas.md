AccelEye has been awarded the EPIC6 cooperative contract for security and school safety products and services in Texas.

A cooperative contract lets eligible public organizations, such as school districts, buy covered products and services through an agreement that has already been competitively awarded, which can simplify purchasing. Under this award, AccelEye's AI weapon detection and related school safety solutions are available to eligible Texas organizations.

AccelEye is proud to be selected and looks forward to helping Texas schools strengthen safety. To find out how to buy through the contract, [contact our team](/contact).

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out. To learn more, visit the [AI Weapon Detector page](/services/ai-weapon-detector) or [contact our team](/contact).
