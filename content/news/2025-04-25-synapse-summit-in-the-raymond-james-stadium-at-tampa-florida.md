AccelEye took part in the Synapse Summit at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida.

At the summit, the team showcased its AI-driven safety solutions, including AI weapon detection with 24/7 human verification, and spoke with attendees about how organizations can add early threat detection to the cameras they already have.

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out.
