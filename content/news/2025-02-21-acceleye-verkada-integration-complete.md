AccelEye has completed its integration with Verkada. AccelEye's AI weapon detection can now run within Verkada's platform.

Organizations that already use Verkada cameras can add AccelEye detection without replacing their equipment. Video from their cameras is analyzed for possible weapons, and detections are confirmed by AccelEye's 24/7 human verification team before an alert is sent.

The integration is part of AccelEye's work to fit into the security systems customers already own. See the full list on our [integrations section](/services/ai-weapon-detector).

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out. To learn more, visit the [AI Weapon Detector page](/services/ai-weapon-detector) or [contact our team](/contact).
