AccelEye has deployed its weapon detection and mass shooting prevention solution in Lynwood Unified School District in California.

The deployment is aimed at improving campus safety. The system analyzes the district's existing camera feeds for possible weapons, and AccelEye's 24/7 human verification team confirms each detection before an alert is sent, so the right people can respond quickly.

AccelEye thanks Lynwood Unified School District for its partnership and its commitment to protecting students and staff.

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out.
