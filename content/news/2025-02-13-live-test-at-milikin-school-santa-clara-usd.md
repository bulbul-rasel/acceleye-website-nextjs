AccelEye ran a real-time live test of its AI weapon detection system at Milikin School, part of Santa Clara Unified School District in California.

A live test shows how the system behaves in a real campus setting: the AI watches the existing camera feeds, flags a possible weapon, and a human verification team confirms it before an alert is sent to the people who need to respond.

The test supports the larger deployment work with Santa Clara Unified School District, where the goal is to help schools spot a threat early and give staff and first responders more time to act.

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification.
