AccelEye has deployed its weapon detection and mass shooting prevention solution in Santa Clara Unified School District in California.

The deployment brings AI-powered weapon detection to the district's existing camera network. The system watches for possible weapons, and AccelEye's human verification team confirms each detection before an alert is sent, so staff and first responders hear about real threats quickly.

The deployment follows the live test held at Milikin School and reflects the district's commitment to strengthening safety for students and staff.

## About AccelEye

AccelEye is an AI-assisted weapon and violence detection platform with 24/7 human monitoring and verification. It works with a school's or organization's existing security cameras, so a detected threat is confirmed by a trained person before an alert goes out.
