AccelEye becomes a Certified Partner of Skyward, helping schools build safer, more proactive learning environments.

Skyward is a student information system used by schools and districts. As a Certified Partner, AccelEye works with Skyward to help schools bring AI-powered threat assessment and digital intelligence together with the tools their staff already use.

The goal is simple: help schools spot warning signs earlier and respond in a more proactive way. Learn more on our [Unified AI Safety page](/unified-ai-safety) or [contact our team](/contact).
